The Craft

Instrument-grade,
made entirely by hand.

  • 01Cast to your ear

    Your ear impression, taken in our Vienna studio, becomes a shell that seals perfectly — comfort and isolation no universal fit can match.

  • 02Tuned drivers

    Balanced-armature and dynamic drivers matched and crossover-tuned for a signature that stays true on stage and at home.

  • 03Hand-laid finishes

    Abalone, foil and pearl worked by hand, then cured and polished until the surface disappears under light.

  • 04Built and checked by hand

    Every pair is listened to and checked on the bench before it leaves the workshop. Nothing goes out until it is right.

The Process

From first question to fitted pair.

Five steps, and it starts the moment you say hello.

Consultation

Tell us how you play and listen. We recommend the right driver setup — right here in the chat.

Ear impressions

Taken at our Vienna studio — precise, and done in minutes. The chat books your slot.

Design

Choose your finish, faceplate and engraving. We confirm every detail before a single tool is lifted.

Handcrafting

Your monitors are built, tuned and checked by hand in our Vienna workshop.

Fitting

You collect them and we check the fit together — and we stay reachable afterwards.
